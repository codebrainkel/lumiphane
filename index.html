Photo to Lithophane Generator

Turn your photos into 3D-printable lithophanes right in your browser. Pick a shape, add 1 to 4 photos, preview it backlit in 3D, then export an STL or 3MF file, send it to your email, or order a finished print.

Choose a shape

  • Round Cylinder

    Wraps 1 to 4 photos into a continuous circular lantern, perfect for nightlights and lamps.

  • Hollow Cube

    A 4-sided square lantern with open top and bottom, displaying 1 to 4 photos around the perimeter.

  • Flat Panel

    Tiles 1 to 4 photos across a flat panel for frames, lightboxes, or windows.

  • Curved Arc

    Tiles 1 to 4 photos along a curve that stands upright on its own.

How it works

  1. Choose a shape.
  2. Upload and crop your photos.
  3. Preview the lithophane with simulated backlight.
  4. Download STL or 3MF for your slicer, email it to yourself, or order a print through Treatstock.
